## What is her background (Kang Tao heir, Avida and Kiril Yang, why she left, joining Maelstrom, illness)?

### Takeaway
Born to Kang Tao executive Kiril Yang and Avida, heir apparent per press, with a terminal degenerative illness held off by implants. She ran to Maelstrom and took on chrome to the edge of cyberpsychosis. Avida's death and Kiril's plan to use her as a host for Capri drive the late season. She survives, loses her chrome, and goes to rehab with Weak as a father figure.

- [PRESS] Conflict: some outlets say "young woman" instead. — [JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/us/talia-cyberpunk-edgerunners-age-explained), [Everything Edinburgh](https://everythingedinburgh.com/?p=30750). These look like loose wording rather than a claim she is an adult.
