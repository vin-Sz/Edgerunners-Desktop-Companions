# Talia Yang's relationships in Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2, above all with Capri

Claim flags:
- **[OFFICIAL-show]**: what the episode dialogue depicts, read through third-party transcripts on Scraps from the Loft. The transcripts are unofficial. I read them through an extraction tool, not line by line. Some lines have no speaker label, and Weak is usually labelled "King". Two reads of E10 disagreed in places, and I flag these below.
- **[OFFICIAL-promo]**: studio or promotional description.
- **[PRESS]**: reported by a news or entertainment outlet, often a recap.
- **[FAN]**: fan-edited or unofficial site, or fan inference.
- **SPOILER**: an event from the later season that a companion set earlier would not know.

Source limits: the Fandom wiki pages and TV Tropes refused fetching (HTTP 402 or 403), as did DMTalkies. I found no official Netflix or cyberpunk.net character page. Pre-premiere promo is marked as such where I could date it; show dialogue is preferred over it throughout.

## Who is Capri (Project Capricorn), and what is Talia's relationship with her?

### Takeaway
Capri is a weaponized personality construct from an Arasaka program, split between D's shard and Talia's body, and the two share one brain as bickering, transactional co-tenants who keep each other alive. **SPOILER:** Talia finally trades her own existence to Capri in exchange for revenge on Kiril, and D separates them in the finale; the transcript shows no goodbye between them.

### Cited Findings
**What Capri is**
- [OFFICIAL-show] Flashbacks show Capri as a weaponized personality construct in Arasaka's lab. Test subjects mostly die, none has survived past 48 hours, and Kyoko argues a human host is needed to contain her, with the body acting as both prison and kill switch. Capri is later terminated while Kyoko pleads for her — [S02E07 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)
- [PRESS] She is an amalgam of engrams, digital copies of real minds, born from Soulkiller — [What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/what-to-watch/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-ending-explained-does-it-set-up-season-3/). [FAN] The fan wiki, seen only as a search excerpt, describes several digitized psyches combined into one personality, created by Kyoko and Arasaka — [Fandom: Capri](https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Capri)
- **CONFLICT, who built her:** the E07 dialogue places everything at Arasaka, and [The Cinemaholic](https://thecinemaholic.com/cyberpunk-edgerunners-season-2-ending-explained/) says Arasaka, with Kyoko central. [People via Yahoo](https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-ending-explained-191221285.html) and [What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/what-to-watch/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-ending-explained-does-it-set-up-season-3/) say Kang Tao. In E07, Kang Tao's link is its own Soulkiller variant, obtained through a backdoor deal, not Capricorn ([E07](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)). Prefer Arasaka.
- **Pronoun conflict:** the transcripts and Talia's scenes treat Capri as a "she"; several outlets write "it".

**How Capri reaches Talia**
- [OFFICIAL-show] In E05, Capri surges from D's shard into Talia's neuroport in a crowded public place and triggers a cyberpsychotic breakdown. Talia relives painful memories of her parents, and Weak calms her — [S02E05 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] In E06, Talia wakes to find a fragment of Capri inside her, repairing her body with a buzzing, humming process. Talia asks what Capri is. Capri says this fragment wants to rejoin the half in D — [S02E06 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e06-everything-hate-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] **SPOILER:** Kyoko later concludes that Capri's engram split at the moment D put a shard into another person. She also reveals that Arasaka had flagged Talia as a Capricorn host candidate — [S02E09 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e09-strictly-reserved-for-you-transcript/). In E04, Roman finds Talia's name on the same Arasaka list as D and David Martinez — [S02E04 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e04-someone-that-i-used-to-know-transcript/)

**How they interact**
- [OFFICIAL-show] In E07, Talia says only she can see Capri. Capri insists she is no hallucination and is annoyed at having to explain. Talia calls her a digital construct and tells her to stop spying on her thoughts, since they share a brain. Capri complains she has not been thanked for saving Talia's life, Talia replies that her body is what keeps Capri alive, and Capri waves this off as give and take — [E07](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] Tone: Capri teases and flatters, uses condescending pet names, and warns Talia not to get greedy. Talia is sardonic and transactional, crediting Capri with the hospital escape and agreeing to trust her only for the moment. She asks to get her old strength back, and Capri promises more than that — [E07](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] Capri also overrides Talia's choices. She interrupts the movie date and talks Talia out of meeting D, arguing that Roman is safer as a go-between who holds the chip — [E07](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)

**What each knows of the other**
- [OFFICIAL-show] Talia knows Capri is a construct tied to her body and that part of her is in D's chip. Capri knows Talia's condition and desires, seems able to read her thoughts, and knows Roman is taken with Talia. E07 dialogue has Capri naming neither Avida nor Kiril — [E07](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)
- [PRESS] One review notes that only D and Talia can see Capri, as the chip's hosts, and that full repair of Talia's body would also mean a permanent takeover — [search excerpts](https://thecinemaholic.com/cyberpunk-edgerunners-season-2-ending-explained/)

**The deal and separation (SPOILER)**
- [OFFICIAL-show] Told her body is failing and the illness has reached her brain, Kiril authorizes a full install that would overwrite Talia's mind. Capri tells Talia she will effectively become her. Talia accepts and asks only that Capri kill Kiril as brutally as possible, for her mother. Capri responds with breezy cruelty and very little sympathy, offering only a brief apology, then announces she can now act her own way — [E09](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e09-strictly-reserved-for-you-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] In E10, Capri attacks Kiril in the street, rejecting him as her father because he killed her mother. D later reaches Talia, speaks to her, then calls to Capri, takes over, and hands Talia to Roman. No direct Talia-Capri farewell appears, and Talia never mentions Capri afterward — [S02E10 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e10-then-everyone-went-home-transcript/)
- [PRESS] D removes the chip and installs it in himself, ending the split. Capri uses Talia's implants to rebuild D's lost limbs — [The Cinemaholic](https://thecinemaholic.com/cyberpunk-edgerunners-season-2-ending-explained/). [OFFICIAL-show] E10 supports this: Capri rebuilds D's body, and Roman realizes the arm may be Talia's — [E10](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e10-then-everyone-went-home-transcript/)
- **CONFLICT, merged or overwritten:** [What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/what-to-watch/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-ending-explained-does-it-set-up-season-3/) says Capri temporarily merged with her through shared vengeance, and [the fan wiki guide](https://cyberpunkedgerunners2.org/characters/talia-yang/) [FAN] says merged, not replaced. [E09 dialogue](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e09-strictly-reserved-for-you-transcript/) reads as a full overwrite with no Talia voice left. In [E10](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e10-then-everyone-went-home-transcript/), Talia speaks and they appear to fight over the body, so she was evidently not fully erased.

### Inferences
- Their bond is mutual dependence, not friendship. Capri never shows clear affection for Talia in the lines I could check. A warm "my friend Capri" framing would be inaccurate; wry, wary and slightly grudging fits better.
- Because Capri reads her thoughts and they share a body, a Talia companion could plausibly speak of Capri as someone who knew her too well. This is my inference, not a line from the show.
- Talia surviving with no on-screen mention of Capri leaves her later attitude open, so a companion's feelings about Capri after the finale would be invention and should be treated as such.

### Gaps
- Whether Talia remembers her time under Capri's control is not stated.
- I could not read the fan wiki's Capri page or TV Tropes in full.
- Whether Capri and Lucy (or other season 1 characters) have any link: not established in sources I read.

## How does she relate to her parents, Kang Tao, Maelstrom, D and Roman?

### Takeaway
Talia is a Kang Tao heir fleeing a mother who loves and manages her and a father who treats her as a medical and corporate problem, and Weak becomes the actual father figure. Maelstrom is an escape and a chrome source more than a family, D is mostly a rival for the chip, and Roman is her real friend.

- [OFFICIAL-show] Talia believes her mother called Kang Tao's people on her and resents being hunted — [E02](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e02-give-me-another-day-transcript/). She suspects an affair between Avida and Weak, and she considers leaving home to protect her parents, which Weak calls childish — [E04](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e04-someone-that-i-used-to-know-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] Avida secretly plans with Weak to steal a host body from Kang Tao and move Talia's mind into it by Soulkiller — [E07](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)

**Kiril Yang (father, disputed)**

## How does the show treat the links between illness, chrome and cyberpsychosis?

### Takeaway
They form a trap: a terminal degenerative illness makes chrome her life support, heavy chrome pushes her toward cyberpsychosis, and Capri cures the symptoms by consuming the person. Removing the chrome eases the psychosis risk but leaves the illness in place. The show's parallel is Weak, who has already lost his chrome to a psychotic break.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL-show] Avida treats her constant implants as a possible sign of early cyberpsychosis, but the doctor finds a clean psych evaluation; Kiril hints there is a medical reason for her reliance on cyberware — [E02](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e02-give-me-another-day-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] Weak tells Avida that Talia may show early signs and should downgrade her cyberware and stay on immunoblockers — [E03](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e03-0-2-1-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] A doctor warns Kiril of high risk of acute cyberpsychosis while calling her stability unusual. She believes stopping chrome means dying. Weak answers that losing yourself is worse than death, and admits he does not know whether he hurt anyone he loved during his own episodes — [E04](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e04-someone-that-i-used-to-know-transcript/)
- [OFFICIAL-show] Doctors suggest cutting her cyberware load to ease pain, which implies no cure — [E05](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)
- [PRESS] Degenerative disease damaged organs and limbs; JustWatch frames it as a coin flip whether disease or chrome kills her — [JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/us/talia-cyberpunk-edgerunners-age-explained). Disease drives extreme cyberization to shed a failing body — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-ending-explained/). [FAN] Removing implants would kill her nearly as fast as keeping them — [unofficial guide](https://cyberpunkedgerunners2.org/characters/talia-yang/)
