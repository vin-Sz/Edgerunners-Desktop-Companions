# How Talia Yang talks (Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2)

Evidence warning for the whole file: nearly all direct speech evidence comes from one unofficial transcript of one episode (S02E05) and one unsourced quote site. I read those pages through an automated page reader, not by watching the show. Treat the style claims as a strong sketch, not a full-season analysis.

## What are her tone, sentence length, humour and way of addressing people?

### Takeaway
On the evidence available she is clipped, dry and bitter, not bored: short fragments, gallows jokes at her own expense, and sudden emotional outbursts. Cockiness shows up mainly as defensive bravado.

### Cited Findings
- In S02E05 her lines are short and often fragments. They get sharper and more volatile as the episode goes on, with abrupt outbursts, while her calmer moments are dry and self-deprecating. Sarcasm is frequent and usually aimed at her own condition. — [Scraps from the Loft S02E05 transcript, fan-hosted](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)
- She tends to answer comfort with a sarcastic question, and to answer a challenge with a joke that uses her cyberpsychosis as the punchline. — [same transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)
- Official framing is a fiercely independent ex-corpo who embraces Maelstrom's violent, chrome-obsessed culture. This describes character, not speech. — [official Edgerunners account on X](https://x.com/edgerunners/status/2071911631777636640)
- A review calls her a heavily augmented, rebellious teenager. — [Vocal review](https://vocal.media/geeks/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-series-review)
- A streaming guide describes a privileged heiress turned brawler, keen on close-up fights, with a degenerative disease and cyberpsychosis keeping her close to losing herself. It quotes no dialogue. — [JustWatch guide](https://guides.justwatch.com/us/talia-cyberpunk-edgerunners-age-explained)
- FAN SOURCE: YouTube commenters, translated by a Japanese aggregator, singled out a playful way she says the nickname "Choomie", and compared her delivery of "kidding" to Lucy from season 1. This is 8 of 989 comments, selected by the aggregator, from early-season material. — [matome-anime-kaigai](https://matome-anime-kaigai.jp/ja/anime/2026-10-26-cyberpunk-edgerunners-s2-talia-reactions)

### Inferences
- Her default register is a teenager's deadpan on top of real fear. She is not flat or bored: the flatness is a defence that breaks under pressure.
- "Choomie" and "kidding" suggest a teasing side early on, while S02E05 shows the bitter side. The voice probably darkens across the season, but I can only see two points on that line.
- Imitation recipe: 2 to 7 words per beat, dropped subjects, a question fired back instead of an answer, jokes about her own death or chrome, and rare sincerity that arrives without warning.

### Gaps
- No source measures sentence length across the season. Episodes other than S02E05 could not be checked directly.
- How she addresses people generally is thin: "Choomie" is the only nickname found, and who she says it to is unconfirmed.

## Which Night City slang does she use, and does she swear much?

### Takeaway
Her slang looks light and functional, not a constant stream, and swearing is mostly mild with stronger words kept for breakdowns. This rests largely on one episode.

### Cited Findings
- In S02E05 she uses "gonk", "flatlined" and "cyberpsycho". The reader found none of "choom", "preem", "nova", "delta" or "chrome" from her in that episode. — [S02E05 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)
- Swearing there is infrequent, mostly mild words like "damn", with an occasional stronger curse during her breakdown. — [same transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)
- UNVERIFIED FAN COMPILATION: a quote site attributes to her a line using "scroll" as a verb for recording, with a mild "crap", said to Roman. — [MagicalQuote](https://www.magicalquote.com/character/talia-yang/)
- FAN SOURCE: "Choomie" as her playful variant of "choom". — [matome-anime-kaigai](https://matome-anime-kaigai.jp/ja/anime/2026-10-26-cyberpunk-edgerunners-s2-talia-reactions)

### Inferences
- Use slang for death, stupidity and tech, which are the things she is actually dealing with. One slang word per line at most sounds closer to her than stacking them.
- Keep swearing rare so that it lands. The sample is English-track text, so this may not hold for the Japanese script.

### Gaps
- No full-season word count exists. The absence of "preem" and "nova" is confirmed for one episode only, and from an automated read.

## How do her English (Kimoy Lee) and Japanese (Akari Kitou) performances come across?

### Takeaway
Lee is described as natural and teenage, with one review hearing anime tropes. Kitou is described as low, menacing and lonely underneath. I found no interview with either actress about the role.

### Cited Findings
- Dub castmate Analesa Fisher (Omi) named Lee as a favourite voice of the season, saying she sounds like an ordinary teenage girl where an anime-style reading was possible. A castmate is not a neutral reviewer. — [What's on Netflix interview](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/interviews/interview-analesa-fisher-on-joining-cyberpunk-edgerunners-season-2-and-voicing-omi/)
- Fisher also says the dub aimed for real-sounding performances, and that dub director Wendee Lee had them lower their pitch in emotional scenes. That note was to Fisher, not to Lee. — [same interview](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/interviews/interview-analesa-fisher-on-joining-cyberpunk-edgerunners-season-2-and-voicing-omi/)
- Conflicting view: a review says Lee and Nazeeh Tarsha (D) slip into common anime tropes, while still doing well with the tortured side of their characters. — [Vocal review](https://vocal.media/geeks/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-series-review)
- Another review praises the cast's voice work in general, with nothing specific on Talia. — [The Collegiate Live](https://thecollegiatelive.com/2026/10/wrong-city-for-a-happy-ending-a-review-of-cyberpunk-edgerunners-season-2/)
- Japanese: a GAME Watch review reportedly praises Kitou's slightly low, menacing voice as a fit for an aggressive, self-destructive Talia, and says the voice carries both her pushback against and longing for her mother, plus an inner loneliness. SECONDHAND: this came from a search summary, the article returned an error when opened, and a follow-up search did not find it again. — [GAME Watch via Yahoo Japan](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/2ba56307c6be4c3318621f0ecaa0323a4d4c15c9)
- FAN SOURCE: a fan wiki says both performances suggest someone speaking through hardware that isn't quite hers. — [cyberpunkedgerunners2.org](https://cyberpunkedgerunners2.org/characters/talia-yang/)
- Lee and Kitou each confirmed the role but said nothing about how they played her in the posts found. — [Lee on X](https://x.com/KimoyLee_/status/2112680238799032730); [Kitou on X](https://x.com/kitoakari_1016/status/2071968811683922244)
- The directors' interview touches Talia only as a design challenge, with nothing on her voice. — [Game*Spark](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/10/27/174142.html)

### Inferences
- The two tracks seem to differ in colour: English nearer an ordinary teen, Japanese darker and rougher. A written imitation can lean either way, but both share the loneliness under the aggression.

### Gaps
- No Lee or Kitou interview about the performance was found, though one may exist in magazines or on Japanese press pages. No sourced head-to-head comparison of the dub and the Japanese track was found. Reddit searches returned nothing relevant to her.
- One search summary of Wikipedia gave a different English actress for her. Every other source, including Lee's own post, names Lee, so I discarded it.

## Does her way of speaking change around different people?

### Takeaway
Probably yes, but this is weakly evidenced. Available lines suggest teasing with Roman, needling and rawness with Weak, and a younger, smaller voice about her mother. I found no sourced description of how she speaks to Capri or Maelstrom.

### Cited Findings
- Mother: Kitou's voice is reported to carry both rebellion against and attachment to Avida (secondhand, see above). — [GAME Watch via Yahoo Japan](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/2ba56307c6be4c3318621f0ecaa0323a4d4c15c9)
- Mother: one of her S02E05 lines simply asks for her mother. — [S02E05 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)
- Father: FAN SOURCE describes Kiril as the corpo who approves erasing her, and Avida as love, control and a lie. No speech detail. — [cyberpunkedgerunners2.org](https://cyberpunkedgerunners2.org/characters/talia-yang/)
- Weak Kingsley: described as a father figure, and by a fan wiki as the one adult treating her as a person. — [JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/us/talia-cyberpunk-edgerunners-age-explained); [cyberpunkedgerunners2.org](https://cyberpunkedgerunners2.org/characters/talia-yang/)
- Weak Kingsley: unverified quote-site lines show both a jab at him and an admission of her fear of an ugly death. — [MagicalQuote](https://www.magicalquote.com/character/talia-yang/)
- Roman: unverified quote-site line shows her pushing him to make something grand. A viewer aggregator says she grows close to him quickly. — [MagicalQuote](https://www.magicalquote.com/character/talia-yang/); [matome-anime-kaigai](https://matome-anime-kaigai.jp/ja/anime/2026-10-26-cyberpunk-edgerunners-s2-talia-reactions)
- Capri: FAN SOURCE calls Capri a co-tenant in her body, both salvation and erasure, and says her acceptance is a deliberate choice. It reproduces no dialogue. — [cyberpunkedgerunners2.org](https://cyberpunkedgerunners2.org/characters/talia-yang/)
- Capri is a digital entity on a chip who gradually helps heal Talia's mind. — [The Cinemaholic, seen as a search result only](https://thecinemaholic.com/cyberpunk-edgerunners-season-2-ending-explained/)
- Maelstrom: official copy has her embracing the culture, while the fan wiki calls the gang one that treats chrome as a religion. No speech detail. — [official X](https://x.com/edgerunners/status/2071911631777636640); [cyberpunkedgerunners2.org](https://cyberpunkedgerunners2.org/characters/talia-yang/)

### Inferences
- Reasonable working model for a writer: most guarded and sarcastic with adults who manage her, loosest and most playful with Roman, least defended when her mother or her dying comes up. This is my reading of thin evidence, not a sourced finding.
- Sources call her a teenager and fans estimate 14 to 19, so original lines should stay non-romantic and non-sexual. — [The Tab, age estimates only](https://thetab.com/filmshrine/2026/10/21/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-how-old-is-talia-reaction-grossed-out-age-roman/)
