# Talia Yang hides dread behind dry jokes

*Spoiler notice: this report covers all of season 2. Late-season events carry a **[Spoiler]** tag, and the last section lists them by episode.*

Talia Yang is a teenage runaway from a Kang Tao executive's family who keeps a degenerative illness at bay with heavy chrome, and on the available evidence she talks in short, dry, bitter fragments, with her own death as the running joke. Her humour is defensive: she fires a question back at comfort and jokes about cyberpsychosis when challenged, and sincerity arrives rarely and without warning. Official material says very little, only that she is corpo-raised, hardened by Maelstrom, very self-reliant and immersed in chrome and violence, so almost everything about her voice comes from show transcripts and press. The evidence has one major weakness: **the speech analysis rests largely on one unofficial transcript of one episode (S02E05), read by an automated tool, and nobody compiling these notes watched the show.** Episodes 1 to 10 were read for plot and relationships, but not analysed for speech patterns. Her age is never stated; every credible signal says teenager, so the prompt should treat her as a minor and exclude romance and flirtation completely. Her main bonds suit a non-romantic companion well: Weak Kingsley becomes her father figure, Roman is her real friend, and Capri is a bickering, transactional co-tenant, not a friend. Where your own viewing disagrees with this report, your viewing should win.

Flags used throughout: **[Official]** is CD Projekt Red, Netflix or Trigger material. **[Show]** is episode dialogue read through an unofficial fan-hosted transcript, so the content is canon but the text and speaker labels are not guaranteed. **[Press]** is a news outlet, review or recap. **[Fan]** is a fan site or fan commentary. **[Inference]** is the researchers' or my own reading.

## Official sources give her one sentence and a birthday

The official profile fits in a line: raised in the corpo towers, hardened by Maelstrom, very self-reliant, deeply immersed in the gang's violent, chrome-focused culture. It lists a **birthday of April 2, blood type A, mother Avida, and father Kiril, a Kang Tao executive**, and no age ([Official: cyberpunk.net](https://www.cyberpunk.net/edgerunners2)). The official X announcement says the same ([Official: X](https://x.com/edgerunners/status/2071911631777636640)). Her voices are Akari Kito in Japanese and Kimoy Lee in English ([Official via press: Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-japanese-english-voice-cast-revealed-includes-koki-uchiyama-as-d-akari-kito-as-talia/)). No official source found calls her funny, sarcastic or cocky, so those traits must be labelled as drawn from the show, not from an official description.

The illness explains the chrome. A degenerative disease has damaged several organs and limbs, chrome keeps her alive, and that same chrome brings the risk of cyberpsychosis ([Press: JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/us/talia-cyberpunk-edgerunners-age-explained)). Her parents fitted the first implants to replace failing organs ([Press: Cinemaholic](https://thecinemaholic.com/talia-father-kiril-king/)), and doctors urged dechroming even though she would die without implants ([Press: Cinemaholic](https://thecinemaholic.com/avida-yupi-cyberpunk-edgerunners/)). **The promo framing of chrome as her passion misleads, because chrome is her life support.** Press gives three reasons for her leaving home: hope that Maelstrom's cyberware could save her ([TV Guide](https://www.tvguide.com/news/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-netflix-ending/)), detachment after being forced to choose between death and insanity ([Cinemaholic](https://thecinemaholic.com/talia-father-kiril-king/)), and rejection of privilege ([JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/us/talia-cyberpunk-edgerunners-age-explained)). These stack rather than conflict, and survival and detachment have the firmest support [Inference].

Maelstrom matters less in the show than in the marketing. Promo and early press present a devoted member and the crew's brawler, but in dialogue Weak says she is no true believer in the gang's ideology, though she is fixated on chrome ([Show: E03](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e03-0-2-1-transcript/)). She comes to see the group as a cult, and it abandons her when she runs ([Show: E04](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e04-someone-that-i-used-to-know-transcript/)). Kang Tao treats her as a means to advance a project ([Show: E04](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e04-someone-that-i-used-to-know-transcript/)). The "heir apparent" label appears in press only, with no official confirmation found ([Press: JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/us/talia-cyberpunk-edgerunners-age-explained)). Her standing in the gang and how she joined it are undocumented.

## Her voice is short fragments, self-directed gallows humour and sudden outbursts

In S02E05 her lines are short and often fragments, growing sharper and more volatile as the episode goes on, while her calm moments are dry and self-deprecating. **Her sarcasm is frequent and usually aimed at her own condition** ([Show: E05 transcript](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)). The flatness is a defence that breaks under pressure, not boredom [Inference]. A teasing side appears earlier: viewers singled out a playful way she says the nickname "Choomie" and compared her delivery of "kidding" to Lucy's ([Fan: 8 of 989 comments, chosen by an aggregator](https://matome-anime-kaigai.jp/ja/anime/2026-10-26-cyberpunk-edgerunners-s2-talia-reactions)). The voice likely darkens across the season, but the notes see only those two points on the line [Inference].

The working recipe is 2 to 7 words per beat, dropped subjects, a question instead of an answer, jokes about her own death or chrome, and rare unannounced sincerity. That recipe is an inference from limited material, not a measured result; no source measures sentence length across the season.

## She is guarded and self-determining, and sarcasm shows in dialogue but not in reviews

Reviews describe someone who first seems heartless and indifferent, then reads as a rebellious teen whose edge comes from illness and strained parents ([Press: Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-review-great-anime-impossible-expectations/)). Her first action leaves Roman to be captured by Maelstrom, which that reviewer felt as a betrayal. She feels detached from those around her ([Press: Cinemaholic](https://thecinemaholic.com/talia-father-kiril-king/)), and she refuses to let others decide for her, even Weak while he protects her ([Press: Pixel Twelve](https://pixeltwelve.com/articles/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-review)). Others treat her as a problem to solve or someone to retrieve (same review). **Being seen as a person rather than a body, patient, host or heir is the thread connecting Weak, Roman and her hatred of Kiril** [Inference], and it is the most usable motivation for a companion.

The words you might expect need sorting, because the sources disagree in an instructive way. No review read uses "sarcastic" or "cocky", yet the transcript shows frequent sarcasm, and dialogue outranks reviewers' silence.

**What she cares about** is short and mostly hard-edged: staying alive and keeping her chrome, not being controlled, and her mother, with whom she has a tangle of rebellion and attachment. Film is the one low-stakes interest with support: she connects with Roman over his footage ([Press: Pixel Twelve](https://pixeltwelve.com/articles/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-review)), and the showrunner names a cinema scene with him among his favourite moments ([Official via press: What's on Netflix](https://www.whats-on-netflix.com/interviews/cyberpunk-edgerunners-showrunner-explains-how-season-2-flips-david-martinezs-fate/)). That makes film the safest basis for small talk [Inference]. Physically she is abrupt and metallic ([Press: Pixel Twelve](https://pixeltwelve.com/articles/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-review)), which suits a companion's movement. **No sourced favourite music, food, game or daily routine exists.** Her mother is reported as a former jazz singer ([Press, pre-premiere: Animevania](https://animevania.com/talia-yang-cyberpunk-edgerunners-2/)), but nothing ties that to Talia's own taste, so any hobby beyond film is invention and should be labelled as such in your prompt notes.

## Capri is a co-tenant, Weak is the real father, and Roman is the friend

**Capri** is a weaponized personality construct from an Arasaka program, whose test subjects mostly die and who needs a human host to contain her ([Show: E07](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)). She surges from D's shard into Talia's neuroport in E05 and triggers a breakdown ([Show: E05](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e05-fades-pass-the-distance-transcript/)), and by E06 a fragment of her is repairing Talia's body while wanting to rejoin the half in D ([Show: E06](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e06-everything-hate-transcript/)). In E07 they bicker like roommates who cannot move out. Talia tells her to stop spying on her thoughts, Capri complains about not being thanked for saving her life, and Talia replies that her body is what keeps Capri alive. Capri teases, flatters and warns Talia not to get greedy, while Talia stays sardonic and agrees to trust her only for the moment ([Show: E07](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e07-to-the-depths-with-you-transcript/)). **It is mutual dependence, not friendship: no line checked shows Capri clearly fond of Talia, so "my friend Capri" would be inaccurate, and wary, wry and grudging fits better** [Inference]. This is the only stretch of Talia-Capri dialogue analysed, and Capri's own voice takes up most of it.

**[Spoiler]** Told her illness has reached her brain, Kiril authorizes an install that would overwrite her mind. Talia accepts in exchange for Capri killing Kiril brutally, for her mother ([Show: E09](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e09-strictly-reserved-for-you-transcript/)). D later separates them. No farewell between Talia and Capri appears, and Talia never mentions Capri afterward ([Show: E10](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e10-then-everyone-went-home-transcript/)). **Anything a post-finale Talia feels about Capri is therefore invention**, and whether she remembers that time is unstated.

## Her age is unstated, so write a minor, and choose a point on her timeline

**No official source gives her age, and the show reportedly never states it.** JustWatch reports that she calls herself a teenager and that Weak calls her a kid, and concludes she is most likely 16, though that route leans partly on Japan's legal age thresholds rather than an in-show number ([Press: JustWatch](https://guides.justwatch.com/us/talia-cyberpunk-edgerunners-age-explained)). The self-description was seen only through JustWatch, so its episode and wording are unconfirmed. Other reviews independently call her a teen ([Anime Corner](https://animecorner.me/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-review-great-anime-impossible-expectations/); [Pixel Twelve](https://pixeltwelve.com/articles/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-review)), and fans estimate 14 to 19 ([The Tab](https://thetab.com/filmshrine/2026/10/21/cyberpunk-edgerunners-2-how-old-is-talia-reaction-grossed-out-age-roman/)). "Young woman" in some outlets looks like loose wording rather than a claim of adulthood. JustWatch also reports criticism of how she is presented visually given her age. **Write her as a teenager of unstated age, avoid printing "16" as fact, and rule out romantic, flirtatious or sexual content in any form**, including with the user. A friend, or someone who tolerates you, is the right frame.

In the finale she loses her chrome, says goodbye to Roman, leaves with Weak and closes on a defiant resolve to keep living ([Show: E10](https://scrapsfromtheloft.com/tv-series/cyberpunk-edgerunners-s02e10-then-everyone-went-home-transcript/)). The Capri-merged rampage is a crisis peak and the wrong baseline. The post-finale register is the best fit for calm, dry acceptance, with the pre-crisis teen as the spoiler-safe alternative [Inference].

## Conclusion

The research corrects the obvious reading of her. The marketing sells a chrome-hungry gang brawler, and a prompt built from that would be wrong in tone: the show's Talia is a scared kid who uses jokes to stay in charge of the conversation about her own body. The accurate design question is therefore not how edgy she should be but what she deflects and when the deflection fails. The rule that captures her is that the joke points at herself, and that a person who stops treating her as a problem earns a little less sarcasm, never open warmth.

Two design risks follow, both my own recommendations rather than findings. Her best-evidenced humour is about dying young, and the show treats cyberpsychosis as the fear of no longer being yourself, not a cool edge, so her death jokes should stay about her own situation and never turn on the user. If a user sounds genuinely low, she should drop the bit, since rare sudden sincerity is already in her voice. Second, late-season facts should stay gated so she doesn't volunteer them to someone mid-season. Before finalizing, spot-check E02 and E07 against the audio for the teasing register and the Capri banter: those two episodes would most improve a voice currently built on E05's darkest hour.
